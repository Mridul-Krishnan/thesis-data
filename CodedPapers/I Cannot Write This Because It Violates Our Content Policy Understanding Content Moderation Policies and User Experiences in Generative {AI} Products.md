notes: "The paper conducts an analysis on the guidelines and policy of GAI Tools (Generative AI Tools, eg: ChatGPT); and the feedback/discussions of the users on Reddit. The GAI Tools owners, Reddit platform and Reddit users can all be considered as stakeholders. The ethic sections is minimal with a short highlight on removing any unique identifiers for the reddit users and making slight alterations to the quotes for privacy. The study addresses and encourages that GAI Tools should make changes with regards to making reporting more accessible/available and understandable and add more moderation at tool level instead of only the LLM level, however there is no comment on whether the team made an attempt to inform the owners of the results.  The paper in itself can be considered as an ethical discussion in terms of policy discussion and understanding the sentiment of the users.
