Ethics section: We conducted our experiments over the wire, using a satellite channel simulator to account for channel effects. This ensured that the experiments caused no harm or service disruption to the wireless services in the vicinity of our lab. Our research does not involve humans; all the data is the result of simulations that do not have privacy implications. The publication of this research has the potential to prevent location spoofing. The only real stakeholders that would be negatively affected by this publication would be the perpetrators of the attacks themselves. The publication of this research has the potential to protect stakeholders that rely on positioning from location spoofing by malicious stakeholders; hence, we consider it ethical.
