Ethics section: This study employs crawled web data as its preliminary raw data. Web crawling itself has been widely adopted by Internet researchers over the past few decades. Most of the data used in this research is sourced from pre-crawled datasets such as CommonCrawl [10] and the Wayback Machine [3]. A small portion of our data is collected through our own web crawls; however, we strictly limit this activity to gathering only the minimum data necessary for our analysis. The scope of our research is confined to examining news articles related to ransomware incidents, and there are no alternative methods available to achieve the required scale for this study.
