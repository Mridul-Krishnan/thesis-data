Ethics section: The DailyDialog dataset used in EchoLLM is open-source and has been de-anonymized by retaining only clear conversations and removing identifiable information. The user studies in EchoLLM were approved with an IRB waiver. No personal information, other than recorded mmWave and audio signals, was collected. The experiments, randomly arranged by the authors, did not involve any private or unethical content. The securely stored data will be used exclusively for EchoLLM and not disclosed to third parties. All experiments were conducted with informed consent from volunteers.
