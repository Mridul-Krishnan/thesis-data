Ethics section: "This section outlines the ethical considerations in designing, implementing, and evaluating LEMIX. The primary stakeholders are:\r

  \r

  • Maintainers of the evaluated RTOSes and embedded applications.\r

  • Users of those systems.\r

  \r

  Risks and Benefits from Discovered Bugs: Our evaluation of LEMIX resulted in 21 new bugs. These defects, though not deemed security-critical, could lead to malfunctions or crashes. Hence, we reported all issues via public bug trackers and submitted corresponding patches. Most were acknowl edged and merged by maintainers. Users who do not update may face residual risks if bugs are later exploited.\r

  Risks and Benefits from LEMIX’s Release: LEMIX will be open-sourced (see §10), making it available to both defenders and potential attackers. Like other defect discovery tools (e.g., fuzzers), it can be used ethically or maliciously.\r

  These risks are common in vulnerability research. Our work aligns with accepted ethical standards in cybersecurity.\r

  To minimize risk, we submitted patches with each report; vendors did not treat the bugs as significant security threats, and no CVEs were issued."
