Ethics section: "Our work does not raise ethical concerns. Our data collection methodology was rate-limited by manual effort and did not generate high web traffic volumes that could overwhelm a studied website. This paper involved only publicly available data, without sensitive or identifying information about individuals or groups. We acknowledge our position as US-based researchers studying guidance from regions of the world where we do not reside; we refrain from passing judgment on whose advice is “best” and focus our investigation on consensus between sources. Our work presents no clear harms, and we anticipate only positive societal impacts: we hope that gathering and analyzing our dataset of global enterprise security guidance can prompt governments to rethink and improve their guidance, and therefore enterprise security as a whole."
