Ethics section: "This paper investigates the privacy threats posed to Vision Language Models (VLMs) by membership inference. This research extends the application of membership inference beyond traditional models to the newly emerged domain of VLMs. In our study, we conduct experiments using publicly available datasets and open-source VLM architectures that are commonly employed in VLM research. The dataset we created is also curated based on a public dataset with the help of large language models. These datasets and models do not pose any direct ethical concerns.\r

  Our work highlights a significant vulnerability in the privacy of VLMs, underscoring the importance of raising aware ness about this issue. This is particularly crucial as VLMs represent an emerging research area that is experiencing rapid development and increasingly widespread application. By identifying such vulnerabilities early, we aim to prompt the research community and practitioners to develop more robust defense mechanisms against these threats. Furthermore, our method can be utilized to detect unauthorized data usage in VLM training, thereby contributing to the establishment of stronger safeguards for dataset copyright."
