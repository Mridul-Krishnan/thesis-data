Ethics section: "We take ethics seriously in this project. All Rust repositories we tested in the paper are publicly accessible on Github. Dur ing evaluations of our work, TYPEPULSE identified several previously unknown type confusion vulnerabilities in widely used software. In each case, we followed a responsible dis closure policy, and reported our discovered vulnerabilities to the developers. All the examples mentioned in the paper are the issues that have been acknowledged and fixed.
