Ethics section: "Throughout the development and application of DELETE TRACKER, we prioritized adherence to ethical principles at every stage of the study and ensured the integrity of our re search.\r

  Data collection: We sourced app metadata from Andro Zoo [28,30], an open-source dataset. Additionally, we collected publicly accessible data, such as account deletion links, data management links from the Google Play mobile app, and the data deletion statement from the Google Play website. No user data, developer information, or sensitive personal data was accessed, stored, or used during the study.\r

  Account management: We registered around 300 accounts in total throughout the study. Each app was assigned two registered accounts to minimize any impact on app systems.\r

  During registration, we used a virtual profile to ensure no real personal information was provided. All accounts were created using new email addresses containing no personally identifiable information. This approach ensured responsible interaction with the platforms studied.\r

  Responsible disclosure: We have responsibly disclosed our findings and the identified account deletion issues to Google Play. Google has filed a bug based on our report and assigned it a Medium (S2) severity level, indicating that they recognized it as a significant issue. Additionally, we have notified app developers about the issues identified in their apps, encouraging them to take action."
