Ethics section: "This study is in the area of privacy-preserving computation with the principal goal of protecting human privacy.\r

  The evaluation process poses no ethical issues, as it utilizes synthetic data sets instead of real human data (which suffices, as the performance of the solutions being compared is determined by the dataset size, independent of the contents).\r

  Following publication,no real-world harms or human rights violations are anticipated, since what we do is to offer an additional approach to perform secure two-party graph analysis. Our framework significantly reduces information leakage compared to insecure two-party solutions, thus protecting human rights. Moreover, compared to existing secure two-party solutions such as GraphSC [43], our method offers greater efficiency while maintaining a similar level of security.\r

  Ultimately, this research is expected to not result in adverse outcomes from both the research and publication processes."
