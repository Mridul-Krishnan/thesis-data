Ethics section: While our institution did not require ethical review, we closely adhered to principles in the Menlo report [76]. We de identified all interview data and removed personally identifying information. Participants were compensated a study allowance of INR 500 (6$) for their participation in the interviews. We informed participants that participation was voluntary and that they could withdraw from the study at any point in time. Since participants discussed their experiences with scams, we assured them that they could refrain from answering questions if they felt uncomfortable, and reassured them that we would de-identify their data and only report de identified quotes. Additionally, since some participants were not familiar with Zoom, we offered to use WhatsApp calls or other technology they were most comfortable with. We also conducted the interviews in local languages, and participants felt comfortable articulating their thoughts.
