  The security goal of our protocol is to introduce a verification mechanism that enables recipients to verify the extent of modifications within the fingerprinted database.\r

  It is fully compatible with existing bit-level fingerprinting schemes while preserving fingerprint robustness, database utility, and data privacy. Developed from an impartial and unbiased perspective, the protocol promotes fair and transparent database transactions between database owners and recipients, although inevitably introducing additional computation and communication overhead.\r
