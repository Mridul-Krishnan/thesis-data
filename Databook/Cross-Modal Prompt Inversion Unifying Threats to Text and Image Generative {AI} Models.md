Ethics section: This research aims to advance the understanding of prompt inversion attacks and the associated security and privacy risks across text and multimodal generative models. The work is intended to raise awareness of these vulnerabilities within the academic community and to support the development of effective, evidence-based defenses. All datasets used in this study are publicly available and were accessed in compliance with their respective licenses and applicable privacy standards. No proprietary, sensitive, or personally identifiable information was used. We have also taken concrete steps to mitigate the risk of misuse. In particular, we withhold potentially sensitive implementation details, such as trained model parameters, that could be directly exploited for malicious purposes. Our goal is to inform and strengthen the security posture of real-world generative systems, not to facilitate attacks. This research was conducted solely for academic purposes and in good faith. We welcome dialogue with stakeholders in both academia and industry to further improve the security and safety of generative AI technologies.
