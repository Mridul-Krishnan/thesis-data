Ethics section: "Our research focuses on developing a Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) algorithm for computing the edit distance in the encrypted domain. Ethical considerations have been central to our study, as the work aims to enhance privacy preserving computation while addressing potential risks and broader societal impacts.\r

  This research directly supports the principle of “Respect for Persons” by prioritising user privacy. The proposed FHE algorithm enables computation on encrypted data without exposing sensitive information, thus aligning with the users’ right to privacy. The data used in our study were either synthetic or publicly available and anonymized, ensuring no violation of individual privacy or consent requirements.\r

  The principle of “Beneficence” was considered by evaluating the potential benefits and harms of the algorithm. The primary beneficiaries are users and organizations requiring secure data processing in scenarios like healthcare, genomics, or financial systems. While the algorithm enhances security, we acknowledge that any cryptographic advancement could theoretically be misused. We mitigate this risk by sharing our findings responsibly, ensuring transparency without enabling adversarial exploitation."
