Ethics section: "We confirm that we have read the ethics considerations of USENIX’25 and fully support it. Our research aims to intro duce a defense mechanism into the hotpatch application pro cess for real-time embedded devices using code-shadowing.\r

  Our initial investigation showed that the memory associated with the hotpatching framework is particularly vulnerable to tampering attacks. In response, we developed a framework to protect against these attacks. All experiments for our evaluation were conducted in a controlled environment, without involving users or real-world systems. However, deploying our work in real applications necessitates rigorous firmware testing, including schedulability and response-time analysis.\r

  While we provide measurement results from our prototype, these values may vary in different environments and implementations and should be considered before deployment."
