Ethics section: Our research points out the potential threat of jailbreaking the safety alignment of open-source LLMs. Those LLMs can then be misused to answer harmful prompts and to generate malicious content. Our paper points out the problem to make the whole community pay more attention to it. As LLMs are trained on vast dataset partially sourced from the internet, that possibility of generating malicious content is inherent to them and not introduced by our work. Consequently, our work does not introduce new ethical concerns beyond those already associated with LLMs.
